How it works

  1. Donor posts

    Title, servings, pickup deadline and a pin on the map. It takes under a minute.

  2. Receiver claims

    Nearby NGOs see it live. The first to claim gets it, and nobody else can double-book.

  3. Food is collected

    Contact details unlock after a claim. Mark it picked up and the impact count grows.

Questions

Is the food safe?

Every listing has a pickup deadline of at most 48 hours, and expired listings disappear from the board automatically. Donors describe ingredients and allergens in the details.

Who can see my phone number?

Only the donor and the receiver involved, and only after a claim.

Does it cost anything?

No. SurplusLink is free for donors and receivers.
